# 10. Glossary

**AMO** After Market Order; queued for the next pre-open.
**ATR** Average True Range; average daily movement including gaps. The unit for stops and sizing.
**AUC** Area under the ROC curve; 0.5 = no discrimination, 1.0 = perfect.
**Bandwidth** Bollinger band width relative to price; low = squeeze.
**Base** A multi-week trading range before a breakout.
**BANK NIFTY** NSE index of 12 banks; the most traded derivatives index.
**Breakeven stop** Stop moved to the entry price after partial profit.
**Breakout** Close above a range or resistance, ideally on high volume.
**Buy-and-hold** Owning the index throughout; the benchmark.
**CAGR** Compound annual growth rate.
**Circuit** Daily price limit on a stock or index.
**CNC** Cash and Carry: delivery product at Zerodha (names differ by broker).
**DII** Domestic Institutional Investors (mutual funds, insurers).
**Demat** Dematerialised account at NSDL/CDSL holding your shares.
**Drawdown** Fall from the peak equity to a trough; max drawdown is the worst one.
**EMA** Exponential moving average; weights recent prices more.
**Expectancy** Average R per trade = win% x avg win - loss% x avg loss.
**F&O** Futures and options.
**FII / FPI** Foreign institutional / portfolio investors.
**Gap** Open far from the previous close.
**GIFT Nifty** NIFTY futures traded in GIFT City from early morning; a pre-open cue.
**GTT** Good Till Triggered: a standing stop or target order across days.
**Hysteresis** Requiring a bigger move to exit than to enter, to stop flip-flopping.
**India VIX** Implied 30-day NIFTY volatility from option prices.
**Intraday** Buy and sell the same day; speculative business income for tax.
**LTCG / STCG** Long / short-term capital gains (12 months boundary for listed equity).
**MACD** Moving Average Convergence Divergence: EMA12 - EMA26 with a 9-day signal line.
**MIS** Margin Intraday Square-off: leveraged intraday product.
**Momentum** Tendency of recent winners to keep winning over 1 to 12 months.
**MTF** Margin Trading Facility: borrowing from the broker to hold shares.
**NIFTY 50** NSE's 50-stock benchmark index.
**Out-of-sample** Predictions made on data the model never trained on.
**Position sizing** Choosing quantity so a stop-out loses a fixed % of capital.
**Pullback** A short counter-trend move within a trend.
**R / R-multiple** Risk unit: rupees lost if the stop is hit; results measured in multiples of it.
**Regime** Bull / bear / range state of the market, usually read off the SMA-200.
**Relative strength** Stock return minus index return (not RSI).
**RSI** Relative Strength Index, 0 to 100 momentum oscillator.
**SEBI** Securities and Exchange Board of India, the regulator.
**Sharpe ratio** Annualised return per unit of volatility.
**SL / SL-M** Stop-loss limit / stop-loss market order.
**Slippage** Difference between the expected and the filled price.
**SMA** Simple moving average.
**STT** Securities Transaction Tax; 0.1% each side on delivery.
**Support / resistance** Price levels where buying / selling has repeatedly appeared.
**Swing trading** Holding 2 to 15 sessions based on daily charts.
**T+1** Settlement one business day after the trade.
**Trailing stop** Stop that follows price up (for longs) and never moves down.
**Walk-forward** Rolling train-then-test evaluation that mimics live use.
**Whipsaw** Being stopped out and then watching price go your way.
